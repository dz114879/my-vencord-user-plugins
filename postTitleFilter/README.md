# PostTitleFilter

`PostTitleFilter` hides forum and media channel posts from the post browsing list when the post title matches one of your rules.

## Behavior

- Rules are global across all servers and forum/media channels.
- Plain keywords use case-insensitive substring matching and support Chinese text.
- Regex rules are case-insensitive.
- Matching posts are hidden only from the post browsing list.
- The plugin does not hide messages inside a post, block direct navigation, or alter unread/mention state.
